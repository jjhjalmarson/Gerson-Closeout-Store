{# What the buyer would sell it for, from what they offer (JJ, 2026-09-03). The buyer sets two numbers once — the freight factor they carry and the margin they need — and every line then answers the only question they actually have: "if I buy it at my price, what do I put on the shelf?" landed = offer / (1 - freight factor) your retail = landed / (1 - target margin) MSRP sits beside it as the anchor, so the gap between the two is the story: what it sold for, and what they could sell it for. Every row carries [data-msrp] and an input.offer (or [data-price], where the price is fixed, as on a counter); the answer goes into td.retail, and td.retail-total, if present, gets the retail value of the whole buy. All of it is their arithmetic, in their browser, kept in their own storage. Neither number is ever sent to Gerson. #}
